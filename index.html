What we do

Waterproofing services built for Tamil Nadu's climate

Monsoon seepage, hard-water tanks, chlorinated pools and factory floors each need a different system. We match the material to the problem, not the other way around.

Roof & Terrace Waterproofing

Stops leakage and seepage at the source with a durable coating system built for direct monsoon exposure.

Water Tank Waterproofing

Food-grade treatment for overhead and underground tanks, keeping stored drinking water clean and safe.

Swimming Pool Waterproofing

Specialised treatment for pool shells and decks that resists chlorine and constant water pressure.

Industrial Waterproofing

Heavy-duty systems for factories and warehouses that stand up to chemicals, traffic and weather.

Crack Repair & PU Grouting

Expansion joint treatment and structural crack sealing that closes off gaps before they widen.

Membrane & Polymer Coatings

Bitumen sheets, fibreglass, thermal insulation and polymer paste, applied to manufacturer spec.

How we work

A systematic process, every time

Four steps that stay the same whether it's a terrace, a tank or a factory floor.

1

Inspection

We inspect the site to trace the root cause of leakage or seepage, not just the visible symptom.

2

Surface preparation

Cleaning, removing loose material and repairing cracks so the treatment has a sound base.

3

Material application

The chosen membrane, coating or grout goes on to manufacturer specification.

4

Curing & testing

Proper cure time followed by a water-ponding test before we call the job done.
