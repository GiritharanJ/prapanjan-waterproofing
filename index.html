Our Waterproofing Process

We follow a systematic approach to ensure complete protection against water ingress.

1

Inspection

We thoroughly inspect the site to identify the root cause of leakage and seepage.

2

Surface Preparation

Cleaning the surface, removing loose particles, and repairing cracks before application.

3

Material Application

Applying the chosen waterproofing membrane, coating, or grouting as per specifications.

4

Curing & Testing

Allowing proper curing time and conducting water ponding tests to ensure zero leakage.
